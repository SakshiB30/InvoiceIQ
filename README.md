## 🌪️ The Real-World Problem

Every business runs on invoices, and every business struggles with them.

- 📚 Invoices arrive as PDFs, photos and scans, scattered across emails and folders.
- ⌨️ Someone has to type every detail by hand, which is slow, boring and error-prone.
- 🔁 The same invoice can be paid twice, and nobody notices.
- ➕ Totals don't always add up, and mistakes slip through.
- 🙈 Money is spent every day, yet nobody can clearly see where it goes.

Small businesses and freelancers lose time, money and peace of mind to this mess.

---

## 💡 The Idea

**What if an invoice could read itself?**

InvoiceIQ turns a messy invoice into clean, trusted data:

1. 📤 **Upload** the invoice (PDF or image).
2. 🤖 **AI reads it** and fills in the details for you.
3. ✅ **It checks itself** for missing fields, wrong totals and duplicates.
4. 👀 **You review and confirm**, so you stay in control.
5. 📊 **You get clarity**: expenses, charts and AI insights showing where your money goes.

---

## 🎯 The Goal

> **From invoice chaos to financial clarity.**

Less typing. Fewer mistakes. Smarter spending decisions.
